Our Story

Two friends, one second-hand roaster, and a very small shop.

In 2014, Maya and Theo bought a battered 5-kilo roaster from a closing café in Portland and squeezed it into a 40-square-metre space on Maple Street. The first month, they sold eleven cups a day. The neighbours kept coming back anyway.

A decade later we still roast every batch by hand, still know most customers by their order, and still believe a good cup of coffee is mostly about slowing down long enough to drink it.

  • Beans roasted in-house every Tuesday and Friday morning.
  • Pastries from a family bakery two blocks away.
  • Direct-trade contracts with nine smallholder farms.
  • Compostable cups, and a 50¢ discount for bringing your own.
